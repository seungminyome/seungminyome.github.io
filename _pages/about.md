I am an M.S. student in Energy & Petroleum Engineering at the University of Wyoming, advised by [Dr. Soheil Saraji](https://www.uwyo.edu).

My research focuses on computational solid mechanics — specifically, modeling how proppant grains interact with and embed into deformable rock fracture walls under compressive loading. I build structured O-grid meshes, implement elasto-plastic Mohr–Coulomb rock models, and validate stress distributions against Hertz contact theory, all within the [solids4Foam](https://solids4foam.github.io) framework (OpenFOAM-based). The longer-term goal is coupling embedment results with single-phase fluid flow to quantify fracture conductivity changes.

Before UW, I worked as a research intern at KAIST (gravity–capillary wave dynamics on elastic films) and interned at Samsung Heavy Industries. My undergraduate background is in Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering at Pusan National University.
