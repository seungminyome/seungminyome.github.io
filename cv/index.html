Research Experience

  • 2025 - present

    Laramie, WY, USA

    Graduate Research Assistant
    University of Wyoming
    • Built an axisymmetric finite-volume model (OpenFOAM + solids4Foam) of a spherical proppant grain loaded into and unloaded from Mohr–Coulomb rock through penalty contact; unloading separates permanent embedment from elastic recovery. All rock inputs are published Montney siltstone measurements; nothing is fitted to indentation data
    • Compared the model with published Brinell indentation (35 N) and with eight proppant-pack embedment tests predicted blind; the pack comparison shows a grain-size-dependent mismatch that neither mesh refinement nor dilation angle removes, reported as a model limit rather than tuned away
    • Showed that residual depth converges slowly with mesh and that the earlier training set carried a mesh bias; now re-running every condition on three mesh levels with Richardson extrapolation and uncertainty bands (≈750 runs on UW’s MedicineBow cluster, Oct. 2026)
    • Fitting a dimensionless residual-depth equation (load, cohesion, friction and dilation angles, grain/rock stiffness) by nested leave-family-out cross-validation; added 60 conditions chosen by a pre-registered balance rule so the equation’s range of validity can be stated factor by factor
    • Pre-registered the test matrix and pass/fail checks (convergence, force balance) in Git before running, and automated the post-processing so every figure and statistic regenerates from the archived runs
    • Trained in triaxial cell operation and DCI rock-compressibility (bulk modulus) testing
    • Planned next: inverse models with prediction intervals to find which indentation measurement identifies the dilation angle; preparing the indentation experiments that will supply the data
    • Designed and built a displacement-controlled micro-indentation rig for P–h curves on rock and metal (parametric CAD, 3D-printed fixtures, 1 mm tungsten-carbide ball) with a piezoelectric load cell and a laser Doppler vibrometer recorded through an NI DAQ in LabVIEW

    • Sole investigator on a government-funded study of air-jet-driven waves on a thin elastic film: derived the dispersion relation with film stiffness and tension and confirmed the Kelvin-wave transition on high-speed video

    • Simulated von Kármán vortex shedding near a free surface (ANSYS Fluent, VOF): the closer the surface, the lower the Strouhal number and the weaker the upper vortices
