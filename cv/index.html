Research Experience

  • 2025 - present

    Laramie, WY, USA

    Graduate Research Assistant
    University of Wyoming
    • Built the data generator: an axisymmetric finite-volume model (OpenFOAM + solids4Foam) of a 1 mm ball pressed into and unloaded from Mohr–Coulomb rock; measured rock inputs are published Montney strength values, none adjusted to indentation data
    • Checked it on three finer meshes against published Brinell tests (22 samples, 35 N): peak depth 15.2–17.1 µm vs. 15.2 ± 2.4 µm measured, not yet converged; a Hertz elastic benchmark recovered only 79% of the load
    • Predicted eight published proppant-pack tests blind (69% mean absolute error) and traced the misfit to grain size: R^1.0 scaling in the model vs. R^0.47 measured, unchanged by mesh or dilation
    • Found that a one-group correlation (h/R = 0.0031 Π^0.72, R² = 0.88 on 8 runs) collapses to R² = 0.11 once rock properties vary, so a single dimensionless group cannot carry the law
    • Replaced it with a joint surrogate: peak depth as a power law in normalized load, modulus ratio, compliance ratio, tan φ and tan ψ; end depth as peak times a logistic recovery ratio; 1.45% / 2.29% nested-CV error, stated as a surrogate, not a validated law
    • Showed by sweeps that the input this law needs most is the one nobody has measured: a 10° dilation angle cuts the end depth 45% and moves the retained fraction from 67% to 44% (measured ≈40%)
    • So the dilation angle became an inverse-ML problem: kNN, gradient-boosted trees, TabPFN and a 1-D CNN on ≈250 new simulations with split-conformal 90% intervals, to learn which indentation measurement pins it down
    • Designed and built the rig for those measurements: displacement-controlled micro-indentation, 3D-printed fixtures through up to 20 revisions, 445 N load cell (0.004 N) and laser vibrometer at 2 kHz via NI DAQ/LabVIEW; fixed their cross-talk
    • Run every campaign through an AI coding agent (Claude Code) with test plans and pass/fail rules committed to Git before any run: 426 runs, ≈1,400 core-hours, every manuscript number traceable to a hashed table and script
    • Put the manuscript through a dozen adversarial AI review passes: retracted a headline −0.8% validation figure, disclosed the failed elastic benchmark, and separated the computed end depth from the physical residual depth
